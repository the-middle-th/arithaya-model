## Public Theme

CreativEX 2026: City Branding & Creative Organization

Design concept: Branding Thailand in a Disrupting World

## Extracted Knowledge

### UNESCO Creative Cities

Creative cities are not trophies or isolated campaigns. They work best as long-term cultural corridors that connect place, people, local institutions, knowledge exchange, and sustainable urban identity.

ARITHAYA use: positions Thai cultural capital as infrastructure for learning, belonging, and national capability.

### Tech Driven Creative Organization

Technology alone does not create advantage because competitors can access similar tools. Advantage comes from how people define problems, create options, use data to choose, test quickly, and learn from evidence.

ARITHAYA use: connects AI readiness to human judgment, experimentation, and performance culture.

### UK Creative Industries Case

Creative industry success depends on systems behind the visible brand: policy, funding, ecosystem mapping, intermediary institutions, networks, experimental spaces, impact measurement, and legacy planning.

ARITHAYA use: gives enterprise and city partners a practical model for moving from creative identity to operating discipline.

### CreativeX Platform Direction

The CreativeX knowledge platform connects a knowledge library, speaker pages, dashboard, downloads, member activity, portfolio creation, and impact evaluation.

ARITHAYA use: strengthens the knowledge product roadmap and impact layer.

## Workshop Flow

1. Thailand Today: define the current image of Thailand through three images and three words.
2. The Disruption: select the forces that matter most for Thailand over the next decade.
3. Future Thailand Brand: define vision, brand personality, core values, and one big idea.
4. Make It Real: translate ideas into action across government, business, community, education, culture, soft power, and technology.
